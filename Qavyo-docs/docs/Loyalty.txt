# Qavyo POS - Loyalty Program System Design
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Vision

Qavyo's loyalty program is **not just a points system**.

It is a **Restaurant Growth Engine** designed to help restaurants:

- Increase repeat customers
- Increase average order value (AOV)
- Bring inactive customers back
- Reduce customer acquisition cost
- Increase customer lifetime value (CLV)
- Reduce dependence on Facebook and Google Ads

Every feature must directly help a restaurant make more money.

> **Rule:** If a feature doesn't increase Revenue, Retention, or Customer Lifetime Value, it should not be built.

---

# Design Philosophy

Unlike many loyalty platforms that focus on customers, qavyo focuses on **restaurant owners**.

Customers earn rewards.

Restaurant owners gain revenue.

---

# Development Roadmap

---

# Phase 1 — Core Loyalty (MVP of Loyalty Module)

These features provide the highest return while keeping the system simple.

---

## 1. Points System

### Purpose

Encourage customers to return.

### Features

- Earn points based on spending
- Restaurant decides earning rate
- Current point balance
- Point transaction history

Example

```
Spend $1

↓

Earn 1 Point
```

### Why

Customers already understand points.

No learning required.

### Why Not More Complex Rules?

Most restaurant owners never configure complicated earning formulas.

Simple systems are used.

Complex systems are ignored.

---

## 2. POS Redemption

Customers can redeem points directly during checkout.

Cashier screen

```
Customer

John Smith

Points

580

Redeem Available

$8

[ Redeem ]

[ Skip ]
```

### Why

If redemption isn't available during billing, staff forget to offer it.

Redemption rate drops dramatically.

---

## 3. Referral Program

Customer invites a friend.

Friend places first completed order.

Both receive rewards.

Restaurant configures:

- Referrer reward
- New customer reward

### Why

Restaurants acquire customers without paying advertising platforms.

---

## 4. Welcome Reward

Example

```
First Order

↓

100 Bonus Points
```

### Why

Encourages customer registration.

Improves first purchase conversion.

---

## 5. Birthday Reward

Restaurant chooses:

- Free Dessert
- Free Drink
- Bonus Points
- Percentage Discount
- Fixed Discount

### Why

Birthday promotions have one of the highest redemption rates.

Very inexpensive feature to develop.

High customer satisfaction.

---

## 6. Point Expiry

Restaurant chooses:

- Never expire
- Expire after X months of inactivity

### Why

Unlimited points become a financial liability.

Expiry motivates customers to return.

---

## 1. Double Point Campaigns

Examples

- Lunch Hours
- Delivery Orders
- Weekend Campaigns

### Why

Restaurants can increase sales during slow periods.

Instead of discounting everything, they reward targeted behavior.

Example

Desserts

3×

1×

### Why

This increases Average Order Value.

Example

Spend $25

↓

Earn Points

### Why

Prevents abuse from very small purchases.

Increases basket size.

---

## 4. Maximum Redemption Limit

Example

Points may cover

Maximum 20%

of order total.

### Why

---

## 5. Referral Analytics

Show

- Total Referrals
- Successful Referrals
- Conversion Rate
- Revenue Generated

### Why

Owners need to know if referrals actually generate revenue.

---

## 6. Digital Stamp Cards ✅

Examples

- Buy 10 coffees → Get 1 Free
- Buy 8 burgers → Get 1 Free
- Buy 5 pizzas → Free garlic bread
- Buy 12 bubble teas → Get 1 Free

### Why Include It?

Unlike generic points, stamp cards are **extremely effective for repeat-purchase businesses** such as:

- Coffee shops
- Cafés
- Bubble tea stores
- Bakeries
- Ice cream shops
- Juice bars
- Fast food restaurants

Customers easily understand visual progress.

Example

```
☕ ☕ ☕ ☕ ☕
☐ ☐ ☐ ☐ ☐

5 / 10
```

Psychologically, customers are highly motivated to complete a partially filled card (the **goal-gradient effect**), making stamp cards one of the most effective loyalty tools.

### Implementation

Restaurants can create unlimited stamp campaigns.

Each campaign defines:

- Eligible products
- Required purchases
- Reward
- Validity period
- Expiry
- Maximum active campaigns

# Phase 3 — Intelligent Loyalty

This is where Qavyo becomes difficult to replace.

---

## 1. Customer Segmentation

Automatically classify customers.

No manual setup.

Segments

- New
- Returning
- VIP
- High Spender
- Frequent Visitor
- At Risk
- Lost Customer

### Why

Restaurant owners don't want to build customer lists.

The software should do it automatically.

---

## 2. Automated Campaigns

Examples

No visit in 30 days

↓

Send Coupon

Birthday Tomorrow

↓

Send Reward

Spend over $500

↓

Upgrade Tier

### Why

Restaurant owners shouldn't manually send promotions.

Automation increases retention.

---

## 3. Smart Recommendations

Example

Customer usually orders burgers.

System recommends

New Burger.

Not random promotions.

Relevant promotions.

### Why

Relevant offers convert significantly better.

---

## 4. Visit Challenges

Examples

Visit 5 Times

↓

Free Coffee

Spend $200 This Month

↓

Double Points

### Why

Gamification increases customer engagement without relying on discounts.

---

## 5. Loyalty Tiers

Restaurant creates custom tiers.

Suggested

- Bronze
- Silver
- Gold
- Platinum
- Diamond

Each tier may configure

- Point Multiplier
- Birthday Bonus
- Exclusive Rewards
- Free Delivery
- Priority Reservations
- Exclusive Promotions

### Why

Creates long-term loyalty.

Customers avoid losing status.

# Features Intentionally Excluded

## Daily Login Rewards

Reason

Restaurant customers don't open restaurant apps every day.

Adds complexity without increasing revenue.

---

## Spin the Wheel

Reason

Encourages discount hunters rather than loyal customers.

Poor long-term retention.

---

## Blockchain / NFT Rewards

Reason

No practical business value for restaurants.

High complexity.

Low demand.

---

## Complex Mission Trees

Example

Buy Sushi Monday

↓

Burger Tuesday

↓

Coffee Wednesday

↓

Receive Reward

Reason

Too difficult for restaurant owners to configure.

Too confusing for customers.

---

# Final Philosophy

Qavyo's loyalty module is not designed to give away discounts.

It is designed to create profitable customer habits.

Every feature should answer one question:

> **"Will this help the restaurant increase revenue, retention, or customer lifetime value?"**

If the answer is yes, build it.

If the answer is no, don't.

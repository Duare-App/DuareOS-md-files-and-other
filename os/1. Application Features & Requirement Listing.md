## Sales Analytics & Reporting

1. Daily sales summary and trend analysis -- MVP
2. Top-selling items report -- Future Plan
3. Customer behavior insights and order patterns
   - **Who is ordering?** New vs returning customers, top spenders, loyalty tier behavior
   - **What are they ordering?** Most ordered items, frequently paired items (e.g., Burger + Fries), cart drop-off items
   - **When are they ordering?** Peak hours (e.g., 12pm–2pm), busiest days, slowest periods
   - **How are they ordering?** Dine-in vs Pickup vs Delivery, App vs Web vs Third-party, average order completion time
   - **How much are they spending?** Average order value, basket size, impact of coupons/points
     _Example Use Case: Identifying that Tuesdays are the slowest day and customers rarely order desserts, prompting a "Free Dessert Tuesday" promo._ -- Future Plan
4. Sales by channel (in-app, website, third-party platforms) -- Future Plan
5. Payment method breakdown (cash, card, mobile wallet) -- Future Plan
6. Refunds and discounts impact report
   - **Part 1 — Refunds Impact:** Tracks every order refunded. Includes total amount, count, reason breakdown (late, quality, etc.), and which items/staff have the most refunds.
     > _Example:_ If "Grilled Salmon" has $340 in refunds across 12 orders, it signals a specific preparation or quality issue.
   - **Part 2 — Discounts Impact:** Tracks every promo applied. Includes total given away, top codes, BOGO vs Happy Hour performance, and average order value (AOV) with vs without discount.
     > _Example:_ Analyzes if giving away $1,200 in discounts actually brought in new customers, or just reduced revenue from people who would have paid full price. -- Future Plan
7. Custom filters for date range, branch, and category -- Future Plan
8. Downloadable reports in PDF and Excel formats -- Future Plan
9. Email summaries to accountant or owner (Daily/Weekly/Monthly) -- MVP
